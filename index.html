Roshan's Monthly Exam

English Language Class

Friday, 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM Total time: 2 hours
Instructions: Read each section carefully. Follow the teacher's guidance for any external websites. Write your answers in the spaces provided. Keep your eyes on the clock for each timed section.

1. Speaking Session

30 minutes   The teacher will ask 30–40 questions from the list below.

Part 1 – About You
  1. What is your name?
  2. How old are you?
  3. Where do you live?
  4. Do you have any brothers or sisters?
  5. What do you do? Do you study or work?
  6. What time do you get up in the morning?
  7. What do you usually have for breakfast?
  8. How do you go to school or work?
  9. How long does it take you to get there?
  10. Do you live in a house or an apartment?
Part 2 – Daily Life and Home
  1. What do you do in your free time?
  2. Do you like cooking? What is your favourite food?
  3. How often do you go shopping?
  4. What is your favourite day of the week? Why?
  5. Do you have a pet? If not, would you like one?
  6. What do you usually do on weekends?
  7. Can you describe your room?
  8. How many people are in your family?
  9. What do you do when you feel tired?
  10. What do you like to drink in the morning?
Part 3 – Hobbies, Sports and Entertainment
  1. Do you like music? What kind of music do you like?
  2. Do you play any sports? Which one?
  3. Do you like watching films? What kind of films do you like?
  4. Did you watch any films last month?
  5. What is your favourite TV programme?
  6. Do you read books or magazines?
  7. Can you swim? Can you ride a bicycle?
  8. Do you like to travel? Where have you been?
  9. What is your favourite holiday?
  10. Do you prefer going out or staying at home? Why?
Part 4 – Past Experiences
  1. What did you do yesterday?
  2. What did you do last weekend?
  3. Where were you at 7 o'clock last night?
  4. When did you start learning English?
  5. Have you ever been to another country?
  6. What was the best day of your life?
  7. Did you go to school when you were a child? What was it like?
  8. Have you ever tried a food you did not like? What was it?
  9. What did you do on your last birthday?
  10. Have you ever won anything? What did you win?
Part 5 – Plans, Opinions and Future
  1. What are you going to do this weekend?
  2. What will you do after this exam?
  3. Where would you like to go on holiday?
  4. What is your plan for next year?
  5. What do you think about social media?
  6. What is the weather like today? What is your favourite season?
  7. What do you think about public transport in your city?
  8. What is your favourite place in your town? Why?
  9. What would you like to learn in the future?
  10. If you had one wish, what would it be?
